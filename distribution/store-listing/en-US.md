# Virya Signal — Google Play listing (en-US)

## App name
Virya Signal

## Short description
Shows, tickets, merch and direct signals from VIRYA — all in one place.

## Full description
Virya Signal is the direct channel between VIRYA and the people around the band.

It keeps the things that matter in one place instead of spreading them across multiple platforms: upcoming shows, tickets, merch, band updates and experiences built specifically for the VIRYA community.

Key features:

• upcoming shows and essential event information,
• tickets and a QR wallet,
• direct band updates and signals,
• merch and selected rewards,
• notifications for updates worth seeing,
• integration with VIRYA Area and the Synesthesia experience,
• direct account settings and account deletion controls.

Virya Signal is not another social feed. It is a focused, fast place for people who want to stay closer to the band and not miss what is happening next.
